Common questions

Quick answers on how Blake works. Anything else, just give him a call.

What areas do you cover?

Blake is based in Whataupoko and works right across Gisborne and wider Tairawhiti, including the coast. If you're not sure whether you're in range, give him a call on 027 250 0842.

What plumbing work do you do?

Leaking taps and mixers, hot water cylinders, toilets and cisterns, bathroom renovations, general maintenance, and pipework for new builds and renovations. bsp also covers drainage and gasfitting.

Do you give quotes before starting?

Yes. You get a straight quote before any work starts, so you know where you stand. For a quick quote, email a couple of photos and a rough idea of the job.

My hot water has gone cold. Can you help?

Yes. Blake swaps cylinders, fixes elements and thermostats, and gives honest advice on what size you actually need. Call for anything urgent and he'll tell you what's possible.

Is it worth repairing, or should I replace it?

It depends on the job. Blake repairs taps, toilets and cylinders where that makes sense and only recommends a replacement when it doesn't. You'll get the options and the price for each.

Do you take on small jobs?

Small jobs are welcome. Slow drains, noisy pipes, dodgy spouting or the list of little fixes you've been putting off. Bundle a few together and get them sorted in one visit.

Can you work alongside my builder on a renovation?

Yes. On bathroom rebuilds and new builds Blake works in with your builder and tiler, prices from your plans and roughs in on schedule so the plumbing never holds the job up.

Who will actually turn up?

Blake does. He takes your call, quotes the job, books it in and does the work himself. On bigger jobs, trusted local trades join in so it keeps moving.

Get Blake on the phone

Call for anything urgent. For quotes, flick through an email with a couple of photos and a rough idea of the job.
